# Vision

Grand Sluggers is a **local-multiplayer arcade baseball game** with a large cartoon roster. It should feel like a sports party game, not a broadcast sim.

The north star is *Mario Super Sluggers* on Wii: nine-on-nine baseball where characters have personalities, parks have hazards, and drafting a team of friends beats drafting nine isolated stars.

This is original IP. We steal *systems*, not names, faces, or Nintendo set dressing. The look (Sluggers weight, original toys): `docs/look.md`.

## Fantasy

You and a friend sit on a couch. You pick captains, fill out a lineup, argue about chemistry, and play three to nine innings in a park that might freeze you, swallow a grounder into a warp pipe, or light the ball on fire. Star skills turn a 2–2 count into a cutscene. After the game, someone is MVP because they robbed a homer with a buddy jump, not because their OBP was 0.412.

## What we are

- Cartoon, readable, slightly oversized characters
- Arcade physics: timing windows, charge, contact quality, exaggerated flight
- Team-building as a mini-game (chemistry graph, captain stars, gear)
- Parks that change how baseball is played
- 1–4 players, same screen, plus CPU fill
- Exhibition first; a challenge/recruit mode later if the core loop is fun

## What we are not

- MLB The Show. No plate-discipline sim, no broadcast camera as the default, no 162-game season.
- A Mario fan game. No mushrooms, no princess, no plumber.
- A live-service with gacha. Characters unlock by playing, not by pulling.
- An online-first competitive title. Netcode can come later; the first version is couch + CPU.

## Tone

Bright, loud, a little mean. Rivalries are funny. Bad chemistry throws should make the room yell. Specials should be readable in a half-second: fire trail, tornado, decoy ball, banana peel on the grass.

## Success for v0.1 (vertical slice)

1. Two captains, opposite factions, play a three-inning game in Harbor Diamond.
2. Chemistry is visible on the team screen and changes a throw in play.
3. One Star Pitch and one Star Swing feel like events.
4. A gamepad swing has a timing window a stranger can learn in two at-bats.

If those four are true, we earned the rest of the roster.

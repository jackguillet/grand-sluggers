# Fly-ball ground shadow (#645)

Captured in HarborDiamond Play mode with Unity 6000.5.9f1, at 1280 × 800.
`Grand Sluggers → Verify Ball Shadow` enters a real 88 mph, 32° launch, −18° spray contact and advances the live simulation, actor presentation and gameplay camera. The captures are at 2.5 seconds (dirt) and 4 seconds (grass). Camera.Render omits the screen HUD; the landing ring remains visible.

The dark disk at screen center is the current ball projection; the yellow circle is the predicted landing. The gate checks live X/Z, grass clearance and hold/release/hide. These captures verify rendering, not a completed half or a human look pass. Jack still judges pop/fly readability and both controller schemes in the standalone preview.

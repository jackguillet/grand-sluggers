Harbor kit empties: Dugouts, Bleachers, WallDress, Town, Scoreboard, Backstop.

Drop `harbor-kit.fbx` here (names: dugout-1b, dugout-3b, wall-panel, wall-ring, fan-stand, fan-sit,
home-plate, bag, mound, foul-pole, warning-track, infield-dirt).
Dugouts are sunken pits with stairs, set back off the dirt (`HarborDugout` numbers).
The lawn is cut at those footprints so the pit shows. HarborKit instances the kit
onto the empties. `home-plate` is the OBR 17″ pentagon (point toward the catcher).
Batter’s boxes are 4′×6′ chalk lines, 6″ off the plate. `bag` is a
4-ft diamond pillow instanced at 1B/2B/3B. Missing name keeps the primitive.
The padded wall is a full loop (`wall-ring` from Blender MCP / `HarborWall`):
outfield fence, then in around the dugouts and plate dirt. No chain-link net.
Center-field bleachers are off. Stands are a horseshoe of small packed crowd
(`HarborStands`) with LF/RF roofs.
Stars stay on the fascia. Do not emit a second diamond. Other parks wait.
Diamond ground (paths, mound, striped grass, foul poles, warning track) is
`ParkDiamond` — HarborKit fills it; other parks reuse the outlines.
Dirt: thin home→1B/3B paths, a mound-centered arc behind 1B–2B–3B (thicker
and more curved than the home legs), bag pads, home circle. Grass Y inside.
`mound` is one smooth hill with a flat rubber table — not stacked cylinders.

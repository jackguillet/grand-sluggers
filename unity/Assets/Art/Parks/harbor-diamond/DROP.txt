Harbor kit empties: Dugouts, Bleachers, WallDress, Town, Scoreboard, Backstop.

Drop `harbor-kit.fbx` here (names: dugout-1b, dugout-3b, wall-panel, fan-stand, fan-sit,
home-plate, bag, mound, foul-pole, warning-track, infield-dirt).
Dugouts are sunken pits with stairs, set back off the dirt (`HarborDugout` numbers).
The lawn is cut at those footprints so the pit shows. HarborKit instances the kit
onto the empties. `home-plate` is a pentagon (point toward the pitcher). `bag` is a
diamond pillow instanced at 1B/2B/3B. Missing name keeps the primitive.
Stars stay on the fascia. Do not emit a second diamond. Backstop stays. Other parks wait.
Diamond ground (paths, mound, striped grass, foul poles, warning track) is
`ParkDiamond` — HarborKit fills it; other parks reuse the outlines.
Dirt ring is `ParkDiamond` slabs + bag pads. The lawn is tiled off `OnDirt`
so 18-ft stripes cannot bury the paths. Kit `infield-dirt` stays a catalog name.
`mound` is one smooth hill with a flat rubber table — not stacked cylinders.

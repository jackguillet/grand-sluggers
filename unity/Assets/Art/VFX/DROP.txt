Catalog VFX events. Each special's slot is its own folder, named the catalog id
(data/art/vfx.json). Drop that special's tell prefab in its folder.

Until a file exists, SpecialFx draws the slot's procedural stand-in, the builder
its "tell" names (SpecialTells.Builders), from the sim's own facts and timings.
A tell reads HUD-off at gameplay distance. No full-screen blinds.
